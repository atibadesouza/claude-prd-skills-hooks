# CPM / RPM Reference Table (baked-in)

The money layer of the verdict. VidIQ does not give CPM, so this table is the source for the money tier and the RPM ceiling.

**How to read it.** CPM is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions. **RPM is what the creator actually keeps per 1,000 video views**, after YouTube's ~45% cut and the dilution from non-monetized views. RPM is the real take-home number, so the tier is set on RPM. All figures are baselined to a **North American audience** (US / Canada). Global audiences run roughly 1/2 to 1/3 of these.

**How it was built.** Synthesized from 11+ creator-economy sources across 2023 to 2026 (vidIQ, Shopify, Fluxnote US tier-1, MilX, OutlierKit, TastyEdits, Lenos, upGrowth, miraflow, plus "Is This Channel Monetized" observed data). Outliers and stale single-point figures were dropped; the top-of-range finance numbers ($40 to 60) are Q4 / sub-niche / top-performer cases, not the baseline.

## Tier legend (classify any niche, even ones not in the table)

- **Premium** — RPM $15+ / CPM $30+. Advertisers with high customer lifetime value (finance, insurance, legal, B2B).
- **High** — RPM $8–15 / CPM $15–30. Business-adjacent or high-intent buyers.
- **Mid** — RPM $4–8 / CPM $8–15. Broad consumer interest, moderate ad value.
- **Low** — RPM $1–4 / CPM $2–8. Mass entertainment, cheap ads, monetize off-platform.

If a niche isn't listed, place it by its nearest neighbor and the legend. State that you're estimating.

---

## Two caveats to surface in the verdict
1. **Seasonality.** Add roughly 20 to 50% in Q4 (advertiser spend spikes), and expect the low end in Q1.
2. **Channel maturity.** A new or small channel earns below these bands until fill rate and authority build. These are the ceiling to grow into, not a day-one number.

Never present any figure as a guarantee. Frame as "creators in this lane tend to earn..." Education, not a forecast.

---

## Quick "what that means" math (for the verdict's money line)
Monthly ad revenue ≈ (monthly views / 1,000) × RPM. Use the low and high RPM for a range.
Example: 100,000 views/mo in a High niche (RPM $8–15) ≈ **$800 to $1,500/mo from ads alone**, before the bigger levers (community, product, sponsorships). Always note ads are usually the smallest slice in a high-value niche.
