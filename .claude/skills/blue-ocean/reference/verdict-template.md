# Verdict Template

The one-screen payoff. Fill every block from the research. Specific beats generic everywhere, real numbers and real search queries, not vibes. Run the prose through `stop-slop` if installed. No em-dashes.

```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
BLUE OCEAN VERDICT — [niche]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💰 MONEY
Tier: [Premium / High / Mid / Low]
RPM ceiling (North America): $[low]–$[high] per 1,000 views
What that means: at [their realistic monthly views] that's about
$[low]–$[high]/mo from ads alone, and ads are the smallest lever here.

⚔️ COMPETITION
[Where it's saturated and where it's soft. Specific, from VidIQ.
e.g. "The beginner tutorials are wall-to-wall. The 'for [specific
audience]' angle is wide open. Top results for [query] are 2 years old."]

🌊 THE GAP
[The underserved demand, with at least one real query people type.]
People are searching "[real autocomplete query]" and the best video
on it is [weak / old / missing]. That's the opening.

🧲 HOW THIS NICHE MAKES MONEY
Ads: [tier, one line]
The real money: [the levers competitors actually run, in order, e.g.
"paid community first (most run Skool/Patreon), then their own course."]

🎯 YOUR ANGLE
[their mashup / edge] × [the gap]
→ "[a positioning line they could paste into their channel bio]"
In this lane you're not fighting for volume, you're the only one in it.

✅ VERDICT: [GO / GO WITH A PIVOT / RECONSIDER]
[One or two sentences on why.]
First move: [one concrete next video title or positioning action.]

─────────────────────────────────────
Directional read from public data + published CPM ranges.
Not an income guarantee.
```

## Notes on filling it
- **Money line** uses the `reference/cpm-table.md` "what that means" math. Always pair the ad number with "ads are the smallest lever" when the niche is High or Premium, because it reframes the whole opportunity upward.
- **The gap block must contain a real query** pulled from the demand scan or VidIQ, in quotes. The specificity is what makes it land as real.
- **The angle line is the hero.** It has to be sharp enough that they want to film immediately. If you can't make it sharp, the intake was thin, say so and route to `/mission-workshop`.
- **The verdict is a real call.** GO WITH A PIVOT is the most common honest answer (the niche works, but not the way they framed it). RECONSIDER is allowed and sometimes the most valuable thing you can tell them.
- Keep the whole thing to one screen. Cut anything that isn't a decision input.
